About Us

MediCare Hospital is a full-service healthcare facility with over 200 beds, 50+ specialist doctors, and modern diagnostic laboratories. Established in 2005, we are committed to providing affordable, high-quality healthcare to our community.

Mission: To deliver compassionate, patient-centered care using the latest medical technology.

Vision: To be the most trusted healthcare provider in the region.
